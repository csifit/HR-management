## Processing instructions and boundaries

The Processor provides the subscribed HRConnector modules for the Controller and acts on its documented lawful instructions, including authorised operations in the application and verified support requests. Instructions concerning transfers must also be documented. If applicable law requires processing outside those instructions, the Processor will inform the Controller before processing unless that law prohibits doing so. The Processor will inform the Controller immediately if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes applicable data-protection law and will seek a lawful resolution.

The Controller determines the employment/payroll purposes, lawful bases, employee notices and retention instructions. It verifies entered payroll facts, eligibility and outputs before payment/reporting. This does not exclude the Processor's own legal/security obligations or excuse defects in its service. Controller and Processor responsibilities for Cozumel's own account/billing processing are addressed separately in its privacy notice.

## People, security and assistance

Access is limited to authorised people bound by confidentiality and trained for their roles. The Processor maintains risk-appropriate technical and organisational measures described in Annex B and periodically assesses them. Changes must not materially reduce agreed protection.

The Processor assists the Controller, taking account of processing nature and available information, with data-subject requests, security, breach response, DPIAs and supervisory consultation. It forwards requests about Controller data and follows verified instructions for the response. It reports a personal-data breach to the Controller without undue delay, with information available at that time and further updates as the investigation progresses. Primary and alternate incident contacts and any operational service targets must be completed in Annex B.

## Subprocessors and transfers

The Controller's prior written authorisation is required for subprocessors. Select and complete the agreed model: [specific approval / general authorisation with advance change notice and a meaningful objection process]. Specify notice period, communication channel, objection handling and unresolved-change remedies; do not leave these implicit. The initial approved list is Annex C.

The Processor imposes equivalent data-protection obligations on each subprocessor by binding agreement and remains responsible to the Controller for its subprocessor's performance as required by GDPR Article 28(4). Any international transfer requires a valid Chapter V mechanism and applicable assessments/supplementary measures. A server's EU location alone does not settle overseas support-access or transfer questions.

## Evidence, audits and service exit

The Processor makes available information needed to demonstrate compliance and permits/contributes to audits and inspections by the Controller or its mandated auditor. Agree practical arrangements that protect other tenants and confidential information without removing the Controller's audit rights.

At the Controller's choice, the Processor returns or deletes personal data when processing services end and deletes existing copies unless applicable law requires retention. Complete export format, secure delivery, deletion timetable, backup expiry and lawful-retention handling before signing. Current application archival is not deletion. Retained data must be isolated from ordinary use, protected and erased when the lawful retention basis ends. Complete the evidence/confirmation process in Annex B.

Contract liability, assistance charges where lawful, termination, governing law and priority with the service agreement: [legal review]. These terms cannot contract away statutory data-subject rights or the parties' mandatory obligations.
