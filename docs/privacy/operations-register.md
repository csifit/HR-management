# Privacy operations register — templates

For Cozumel SRL and, in separate copies, each customer controller. **Keep completed registers in an access-controlled operational system, not this source repository.** These empty templates contain no customer records. Assign a responsible person/deputy and establish a retention period for each register. Do not put passwords, diagnoses, full identity documents or employee exports into free text.

## Processing records

For each customer-processing activity record: controller and contact; processor and contact; processing categories; systems/data flows; people and data categories; authorised recipients/subprocessors; countries and transfer safeguards; general technical/organisational measures; instructions/DPA reference; retention/exit arrangements; review date. Maintain a separate controller record for Cozumel's billing, business contacts and support purposes including lawful bases and transparency notices. Regular HR processing is not merely occasional.

## Request log

For requests concerning existing customer employee records, migration 014 now supplies an HR-only in-app register and review exports. Use the [privacy workflow](../privacy-workflows.md), verify the deployed flow and record actual responses separately from export preparation. Keep this separate template/process for provider-controller requests, applicants, other people and cases outside the application's scope. Never copy completed request logs or export packages into Git.

Record ticket, organisation, verified requester, instruction, permitted scope, approver, operator, start/expiry, access evidence, exported copies, revocation and closure. No standing customer impersonation or global HR access is created by this procedure.

## Exit and recovery evidence

Record the authorised instruction, scope inventory, export manifest/counts, secure recipient/delivery and receipt, holds, approved deletion plan, database/file/support-copy completion, backup expiry and final confirmation. Restore tests must cover database and document binaries and verify tenant boundaries after restore. Record actual measured recovery times and any data-loss window before promising service targets.

## Adoption decisions still required

- Named accountable owner/deputy and support/incident availability.
- Approved DPA and privacy notice; confirmed Cozumel registration details.
- Vendor contracts, locations and transfer assessments.
- Category retention and deletion/backup procedures; a tested rights export.
- Risk/DPIA/DPO assessment, access-review cadence, training and restore exercises.
