For each engaged legal entity record: contract/version/date, processing and support countries, categories shared, security evidence, relevant adequacy coverage or SCCs and transfer assessment, customer authorisation, change-notice channel, reviewer/date and next review. Avoid storing credentials or full account exports in this register.

Proposed change procedure: evaluate a vendor/change before enabling it, update this register and customer disclosures, give the agreed advance notice, resolve valid objections, then record approval and activation. Do not describe a supplier as certified or EU-only without evidence. International transfer safeguards also apply where overseas support can access personal data. [EDPB transfer guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/international-data-transfers_en).
